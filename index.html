RS3 Casket Recorder

OBS Replay Buffer continuously retains the recent game footage, including BEFORE you open a casket. Alt1 saves a replay only after a verified Treasure Trails reward, never just a Puzzle casket click. Rerolls and subsequent rewards postpone saving until five seconds after the latest event. No RuneScape inputs are generated.

OBS Replay Buffer — pre-opening footage

Not checked

One-time OBS setup: Settings → Output → Enable Replay Buffer, then set Maximum Replay Time = 60 seconds. You can use another value, but set the same number below. Replay footage is saved to your OBS recording folder. The app starts the buffer automatically while OBS is connected.

OBS saves the entire replay window (up to 60 s), not a trimmed five-second clip. Long sequences are split into potentially overlapping replay saves before the earliest footage could expire. Do not close this window during a pending reward save. Replay Buffer continuously encodes footage, which may add GPU load.

Optional · Automatically extend recordings on rerolls

With a Treasure Trails reward window open, click Calibrate reroll button (3 seconds), then hover over the CENTER of the Reroll button without clicking. The position is saved. Each player click near that button, while the reward window is recognized, moves the replay save deadline to five seconds after that click. No new OBS recording is started.
